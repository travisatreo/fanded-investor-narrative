Today Fanded
turns five.

Five lessons from five years building the first AI infrastructure for the talent industry — and the world we are building toward in the next five.
01
Crypto gave us the playbook.
NFTs extracted from talent — but crypto proved a system could move value between fans and talent without an institution in the middle. Fanded and MAWD were born from those ashes. For the first time, the technology to build power for talent is accessible to teams who know how to use it.
02
Artists are solo founders of global industries.
Every working artist is the PM, the executive, and the marketer of a global corporation, with no capital to hire a team. 200M solo creators are burning out trying to handle it alone. Agentic AI is the only thing that scales the back office of a one-person business to match the front office.
03
The fan list is the line between talent and creators.
Creators can be talent, but talent does not want to be creators. The fan list is the only asset that compounds in the artist’s favor across a career. Fans pay on a recurring rail because they are thanking the talent for the gift — not paying for services. That is our distinction from every productivity platform.
04
The liability is the moat.
MAWD privatizes the most sensitive business data an artist owns. Horizontal AIs cannot copy us because they will not take on the liability of holding it. Fanded must be built B2B2C with security at or above bank-grade. The liability others avoid is exactly where our leverage compounds.
05
Talent is the future of work.
We are the first team to refuse to see talent as an asset to extract from. Gen Z and Gen Alpha are choosing entrepreneurship as a career, not falling into it. Fanded’s systems will power the next 50 years of solo-founder work — for creators, athletes, actors, musicians, and every category not yet named.
