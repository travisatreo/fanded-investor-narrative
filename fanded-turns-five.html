Lesson one
01 · What crypto actually gave us

Fans don't need a security or an NFT to be fans. But crypto is the reason Fanded exists.

NFTs made non-fans wealthy and extracted from the talent yet again. That's the part most people remember. But there was something underneath it that mattered far more than any individual token, and it's the part the industry has refused to talk about honestly.

NFTs and crypto showed the world an entirely new system that could benefit the talent and the fans without needing an institution or a centralized authority in the middle. That was the sea change. The crypto boom gave way to the idea of Fanded existing — a world ruled by independent artists and talent owning their own distribution.

Fanded and MAWD came from the ashes of the crypto industry. We took the belief that survived the wreckage: that we can build the next technological infrastructure for the talent industry.

For the first time in history, technology to build power for talent is accessible to the teams who know how to harness it for the betterment of the arts and the artists. That window is what we are building inside of.

Lesson two
02 · The solo founder problem

Artists are solo founders of global industries. They cannot run them alone.

Every working artist is operating a global corporation by themselves. They have no capital to afford a team. They are the product manager and the executive and the marketer and every other role at once. No one person can handle all of those operations alone, and the data on burnout shows what happens when they try.

Agentic AI is what we believe solves this for the 200M solo creators experiencing burnout trying to handle everything themselves.

They're losing money because they don't know their value in the market. No one has ever synthesized their numbers for them against industry standards — and no single human manager could do it for them at the depth that AI now can. That gap, the distance between what an artist is worth and what an artist knows they're worth, is one of the largest hidden tax on talent in the world today.

Fanded exists to close it.

Lesson three
03 · The fan list separates talent from creators

Talent is not the same as creators. And the fan list is the line between them.

While creators can be talent, talent has told us many times that they do not want to be creators. Professional athletes, actors, and musicians create value not in video form. The need for reliable income from the fans we affect with our creativity is what's been missing from the talent industry for decades.

This makes the most important asset the talent has their fan list and fan data. This is our moat. When talent owns their fan list, they own all the leverage. They no longer have to create for the algorithm because they know exactly who they're creating for. They don't have to rely on algorithmic distribution because they can send their latest creations directly to their fans around the world.

The economics of 1,000 true fans was already proven before Fanded. What we've discovered with this is even more staggering: fans continue to support on a recurring monthly subscription rail as long as the talent keeps doing their passion.

Fans have explained to us they are thanking the talent for the gift they give to the world, not paying for services. This is our distinction.

All other platforms enable productivity in exchange for income. This is not us. Talent is the most sought-after vocation because talent creates once, and as long as the creation can be delivered to the right fans, it continues to generate value without the talent ever needing to touch it again.

Lesson four
04 · The liability is the moat

MAWD turns the fan list intelligent. The liability of holding it privately is what no other AI will touch.

MAWD is the product: turning the fan list, the fan database, and every other information and business source the artist has into a single intelligent system. The product is clear. The moat is the part most people miss.

Our moat is talent trusting us with their most sensitive business data, and us privatizing that data on their behalf. That privatization is what makes it impossible for horizontal AIs to copy us. To compete, they would have to take on the same liability we are taking on — the private data, the business data, the fan data the artists aggregate over a lifetime — and assume the same responsibility.

We do, because no one else is willing to. That is exactly where we believe the largest value and leverage is.

This is why Fanded must be built not as a consumer play but as a B2B2C platform with security as good as or better than a bank's. We are always exploring new security technologies to continue earning the trust our clients deserve. The day we stop earning that trust is the day the moat disappears.

Lesson five
05 · Talent as the future of work

We are the first team to refuse to see talent as an asset to extract from. We see talent as the future of work.

Social media and remote work have created an entirely new generation. Gen Z and Gen Alpha are leaving the old tracked world behind and focusing on entrepreneurship as the career they want. They are choosing it, not falling into it.

Without the tools that Fanded is building, it will be extremely difficult for any talent to thrive in that future — and harder still for the next generation to want to follow them into it. The model has to be visible and survivable, or the people behind them won't try.

Fanded and our systems will power the next 50 years of the future of work. Not just for creators. For all solo founders.

That is the bet. Talent is the most leveraged form of human work that has ever existed — and for the first time in history, the infrastructure to support it is something we can actually build.
