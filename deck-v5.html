The regulatory moat

Fanded is the legal infrastructure that makes fan ownership possible.

For 100 years, platforms owned the fan relationship by default. GDPR and CCPA changed that. Fans now have the legal right to their own data. Platforms have 30 days to comply. Fanded is built on top of this shift.
Step 1
Fan owns their data

Step 3

Data Layer
Fan behavioral data, business communications, commerce-creative correlation. Three proprietary streams outside the public internet. No foundation model can train on it.
Graph Layer
Unified across Shopify, Spotify, YouTube, Mailchimp, Stripe. The relationships between data points only exist when unified. The graph is the product.
Trust Layer
Talent owns the data. Fanded operates it under license. Fans can revoke at any time. Every privacy regulation strengthens the moat.
Spotify (the hedge fund)
• Owns the fan data
• Generates revenue from selling it
• Cannot let fans port data without cannibalizing the business
• $52M in fan data sales last year
Fanded (the broker-dealer)
• Talent owns the data
• Generates revenue from operating infrastructure
• Built to let fans port data freely
• Compounds with every privacy law that passes
“Spotify is the hedge fund of fan data. Fanded is the broker-dealer. Talent is the client. The structure determines who scales.”
Fanded is the data processor. Talent is the data controller. Fans are the data subjects. That trust framework is unblockable by incumbents because they would have to dismantle their own business to copy it.
